# Creator funding and residual recovery

ABI 0.5.0 supports two publication methods. `create` opens the three budgets to public contributions. `create_funded` atomically contributes the entire configured seat capital, prize budget and execution budget from the creator. The creator signs all token transfers. Zero optional budgets create no empty deposit. Each contribution retains its original owner and existing claim/refund rules. Full buckets reject additional contributions. External settlement coverage is separate and needs the named provider’s consent to the exact round terms.

A successfully closed managed vault belongs to its player, including unrelated idle tokens and separately claimed protocol incentives. A deadline purchase gives its residual position to the recorded coverage provider. Both outcomes enter `owner_position_count` and `owner_positions_page(owner, offset, limit)`; limit is at most 20. `position_count` and `positions_page` provide bounded whole-round history. Open positions remain under round control and do not appear as recoverable owner positions.

Clients verify each immutable vault’s code hash and current `info` before recovery. `native_exit` can redeem an active residual position when the external venue works; `recover(asset)` transfers that token only to the current owner. Recovery cannot redirect funds to an arbitrary recipient. LP owners can recover LP tokens without using a frozen router. Blend supply shares remain inside their per-position vault until the pool permits withdrawal. Ownership and cash settlement do not make a frozen external venue liquid.

Persistent indices and child vaults need lease maintenance/restoration. The seven-day lease target is bounded; `keep_position(id)` renews a closed position, its owner page and vault as well as an open position. Retaining a record in an application database does not renew chain storage. Permissionless executors settle to fixed recipients and do not require a new player signature.
